# UI usability corrections — November 8, 2026

The owner’s later review supersedes the fixed-height, independently scrolling dates pane in the [earlier interaction pass](2026-11-08-interaction-refinements.md). This pass corrects the notification lifetime, photo captions, source-title duplication, dates expansion and hero/navigation paint behavior.

## Reproduction and correction

- A completed removal notification still existed after 5.2 seconds on the old production build. Completed feedback now expires after four seconds. A subsequent action restarts that lifetime; an account write remains identified as pending until it finishes. The shared status works for photo bookmarks, solo Tickets and the dialog.
- The real seven-pick Tame Impala example placed white titles over bright photos. Captions now participate in card sizing, reserve space for the top controls, and have a dark backing extending above their first line. This retains the count-based Poster and full source text. Light/dark screenshots cover King Gizzard, DIIV and All Points East. The captured public `/examples` response is a domain fixture, not invented vendor JSON or a new model request.
- Two data tests reproduced “King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard” repeating the Artist spelled with “and”, in both directions. Artist removal now accepts those equivalent conjunctions; only the remaining source title is displayed. Existing tour/festival-title tests continue to protect meaningful titles.
- The old eight-date dialog stayed 491px tall while its details needed 767px. A controlled, accessible accordion now animates its natural height over 500ms. The sheet follows that height; desktop photos retain their opening dimensions and crop, centered in a matte column. Phones smoothly reduce photo height and place Tickets and Save side by side. The standard eight-date case fits without a details scrollbar. Shorter viewports and longer source records retain a reachable overflow fallback and an independent Close button. Collapsed links leave keyboard navigation immediately.
- Settled hero glyphs were misaligned by about 4px: a more specific library rule restored `calc(200% + 4px)` after the application’s half-height override. The scoped long-flap override now wins and aligns the complete glyph coordinates. The same library injected a separate background-color transition on stationary faces, causing their palette to lag the page. Scoped faces now change palette with the page. The researched split-flap library, clipped fold, Anton font and existing colors remain.
- On a delayed home-example response, the green guide initially painted around 550px from the top, inside an 800px viewport, before the arriving strip pushed it away. The guide now waits for the strip’s request to settle; failure or a five-second timeout still reveals it. Hero/input interaction does not wait for this request. There is no extra Search or model call.
